Federal Proposal Compliance Matrix

Turn a live government solicitation into a writer-ready requirement register before proposal drafting starts. BidWorth Research extracts the instructions, evaluation factors, deliverables, forms, deadlines, amendment controls, and response requirements into a structured compliance matrix.

Request a fixed-scope matrix

Best fit: a specific RFP, RFQ, RFI, Sources Sought, or combined synopsis/solicitation with accessible current documents. This is research and document-control support, not a representation that BidWorth has performed or won government contracts.

Deliverable

  • Current notice and amendment control check
  • Requirement-by-requirement compliance matrix
  • Submission instructions, deadline, page/format and file requirements
  • Evaluation-factor mapping
  • Required forms, certifications, attachments and representations
  • Technical, management, staffing, past-performance and pricing input checklist where the solicitation requires them
  • Open questions, contradictions, missing inputs and disqualifier flags
  • Final pre-submission QA checklist

Evidence controls

SAM bulk data and third-party indexes are discovery aids only. For federal work, the controlling current official solicitation record, attachments and amendments must be checked before the matrix is treated as final. BidWorth does not invent past performance, certifications, clearances, personnel, pricing, contract wins, or corporate experience.

What the client supplies

Send the solicitation number or official link plus any downloaded amendments/attachments that are not publicly accessible. If proposal drafting support is also requested, client-provided factual company information remains the source of truth for qualifications and experience.

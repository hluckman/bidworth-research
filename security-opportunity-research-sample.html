Why this is useful: A raw portal alert says an opportunity exists. A qualified research brief explains whether it is realistically pursuable, what could disqualify the contractor, and what must happen next.

What a paid brief can include

1. Discover

Search federal, state and local sources for relevant security solicitations, recompetes and subcontracting paths.

2. Verify

Confirm live status, deadline, scope, attachments, amendments and official contact information.

3. Qualify

Screen location, licenses, insurance, bonding, staffing, set-asides, experience requirements and mandatory meetings.

4. Prioritize

Classify each item as Prime, Subcontractor, Teaming or No-Go with a concise reason and next action.

Example delivery fields

Solicitation ID · agency · official link · procurement type · response deadline · location · NAICS/PSC · set-aside · service scope · mandatory events · licensing/insurance/bonding constraints · entity eligibility check · prime/sub/team/no-go classification · fit rationale · unresolved risks · next action.

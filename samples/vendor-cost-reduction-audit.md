# Vendor Cost-Reduction Research Audit — Sanitized Sample

**Purpose:** demonstrate a bounded, evidence-first procurement research deliverable for a client who wants to reduce vendor spend without outsourcing commercial authority or contract-signing decisions.

## 1. Client inputs

- Vendor list and category
- Current price, unit, quantity/frequency, and renewal date
- Current contract/order form/SOW if available
- Required service levels and non-negotiable requirements
- Known switching constraints
- Geographic, regulatory, security, or certification requirements

Missing information is marked **UNKNOWN** rather than inferred.

## 2. Baseline normalization

For every vendor line, normalize:

| Field | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Current unit price | Invoice, quote, PO, or contract |
| Effective annual spend | Quantity/frequency × evidenced price |
| Term / renewal | Executed agreement or current order form |
| Termination / notice window | Contract clause |
| Service scope | SOW / product specification |
| Fees / escalators | Contract or invoice |
| Switching cost | Client-confirmed or documented |

The audit separates **verified**, **client-stated**, and **unverified** facts.

## 3. Market benchmark workflow

1. Translate each incumbent scope into a vendor-neutral requirement.
2. Identify credible alternative suppliers from official manufacturer/vendor sources and reputable directories.
3. Verify capability against mandatory requirements before comparing price.
4. Record comparable public pricing where available; otherwise mark price as quote-required.
5. Normalize units, minimums, freight/setup charges, term length, and service scope.
6. Reject false savings caused by missing features, lower quantities, teaser pricing, or incompatible terms.

## 4. Savings-opportunity register

| Opportunity | Evidence | Gross savings | Switching/implementation cost | Confidence | Owner action |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Duplicate service | Billing + scope overlap | TBD | TBD | High/Med/Low | Validate business need |
| Alternative supplier | Capability + comparable quote | TBD | TBD | High/Med/Low | Approve RFQ/negotiation |
| Renewal leverage | Renewal date + benchmark | TBD | TBD | High/Med/Low | Approve negotiation position |
| Volume consolidation | Spend history | TBD | TBD | High/Med/Low | Confirm demand forecast |
| Contract-term issue | Executed clause | TBD | TBD | High/Med/Low | Legal/owner review |

No savings figure is presented as real until the baseline and comparison are supported by evidence.

## 5. Supplier shortlist

Each candidate is scored only on supported facts:

- Capability fit
- Comparable price / quote status
- Lead time
- MOQ / commitment
- Location and logistics
- Required certifications/qualifications
- Responsiveness
- Commercial constraints
- Evidence quality

A supplier with an attractive price but an unverified mandatory requirement remains **CONDITIONAL**, not recommended.

## 6. Negotiation-prep handoff

BidWorth can prepare the factual leverage package:

- incumbent spend baseline;
- renewal/notice dates;
- competing supplier evidence;
- normalized quote comparison;
- documented service gaps;
- target questions and unresolved assumptions.

Final pricing positions, negotiation authority, legal interpretation, acceptance of commercial terms, and contractual commitments remain with the client/owner.

## 7. Deliverable

The final client package contains:

1. executive savings map;
2. normalized spend baseline;
3. supplier/market benchmark table;
4. evidence-linked alternative shortlist;
5. contract/renewal issue register;
6. RFQ or negotiation-prep questions;
7. decision log showing what is verified, pending, or owner-controlled.

This sample intentionally contains no invented client history, savings claims, supplier quotes, certifications, or procurement wins.

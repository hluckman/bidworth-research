## Immediate bid/no-bid gates

A prospective bidder should not advance until these facts are established from bidder-controlled evidence:

1. Legal entity and SAM registration status.
2. Ability to perform the actual researcher assessment, engagement, analysis, and consulting scope described in the controlling SOW.
3. Availability and qualifications of proposed personnel.
4. Relevant past-performance evidence sufficient for the solicitation's questionnaire/evaluation requirements.
5. Ability to price the required labor structure using the current J2 workbook without unsupported assumptions.
6. Ability to satisfy all representations, certifications, submission instructions, and contractual terms in the current solicitation.
7. Confirmation that no amendment has changed the September 9 question deadline, September 28 proposal deadline, evaluation scheme, forms, or scope.

A missing answer is an evidence gap, not a fact to infer.

## Compliance extraction workflow

For a client engagement, BidWorth would convert the controlling package into a requirement matrix with these columns:

| Requirement | Source/page | Mandatory? | Owner | Evidence/input needed | Draft status | Final QA |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Submission deadline/method | Current solicitation | Yes | Bid lead | Official instructions | Open | Open |
| Technical response | Solicitation/SOW | Yes | Technical | Factual approach + personnel inputs | Open | Open |
| Labor pricing | J2 | Yes | Pricing owner | Approved rates/assumptions | Open | Open |
| Past performance | J3/solicitation | As required | Bid lead | Verifiable references/projects | Open | Open |
| Reps/certs/forms | Solicitation | As required | Authorized signer | Entity facts/signatures | Open | Open |
| Amendments | SAM.gov | Yes | Bid lead | Latest official package | Open | Open |

No past performance, staffing credential, rate, certification, or corporate experience is drafted as fact until supplied or independently verifiable.

## High-value early questions

Before September 9, the bidder should review the package for genuine ambiguities affecting scope, staffing, deliverables, evaluation, pricing, data access, government-furnished resources, or task-order assumptions. Questions should be grounded in the solicitation text; BidWorth should not generate speculative questions merely to create activity.

## Final QA standard

Immediately before delivery/submission support:

- re-open the official notice;
- confirm latest amendment number and attachment set;
- confirm exact deadline and time zone;
- reconcile every SHALL/MUST submission item;
- verify filenames/formats/page limits;
- verify every factual bidder claim against supplied evidence;
- ensure J2 formulas/units/rates reconcile to the narrative and approved pricing;
- ensure J3/reference information is factual and authorized;
- flag all signatures, certifications, legal representations, and price approvals for the bidder's authorized owner.

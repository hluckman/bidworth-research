## 7. Pricing-risk checklist

Before a quote is finalized, explicitly test for omitted cost in:

- freight and island logistics;
- staging, lifts/scaffolding and access equipment;
- lumber/framing and corrugated roofing materials;
- fasteners/connectors suitable for required wind/weather exposure;
- pest-prevention components;
- prevailing-wage labor and payroll burden;
- permits/licenses if applicable;
- cleanup and disposal;
- inspection/punch-list return work;
- warranty exposure;
- schedule contingency for material availability and weather.

BidWorth should organize and verify these inputs; the contractor retains pricing authority and responsibility for the quote.

## 8. Rapid recommendation framework

**GO** only if eligibility, technical capability, Hilo execution, schedule, and required experience are evidenced and the current solicitation package has been reconciled.

**CONDITIONAL** if the contractor appears capable but one or more controlling documents, eligibility facts, site assumptions, or cost inputs remain unresolved.

**NO-BID** if the contractor cannot truthfully satisfy the set-aside/registration requirements, required experience, technical scope, schedule, or material labor/logistics economics.

## 9. Final QA

Before submission, independently check solicitation number, amendment acknowledgments, deadline/time zone, offeror identity/UEI, representations, technical response, experience evidence, price math, wage assumptions, signatures, filenames, submission address/method, and proof of timely transmission.

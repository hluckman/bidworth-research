Travis AFB Portable Fire Extinguisher RFQ

FA442726Q1153 Total Small Business NAICS 339999 Due Sep. 18, 2026

Executive screen

Status: Active solicitation. The Air Force is procuring portable fire extinguishers at Travis AFB, California. Offers are due September 18, 2026 at 12:00 PM Pacific. The solicitation anticipates a Total Small Business Set-Aside.

Near-term action: A capable small fire-protection supplier should review the full solicitation package now, validate every equipment/specification requirement, and resolve any open questions before the RFI cutoff.

BidWorth review checklist

  • Confirm current SAM registration and small-business eligibility for the applicable NAICS.
  • Extract every extinguisher type, quantity, rating, certification, delivery, and acceptance requirement from the SOW.
  • Reconcile solicitation clauses against the contractor-responsibility form and security requirements.
  • Track any amendment or RFI response issued before quote close.
  • Build a submission checklist and verify that the final email goes to every required government recipient before the deadline.

Prospect-fit example

A Northern California fire-protection contractor with portable-extinguisher sales/service capability, active federal registration, small-business status, and prior federal fire-extinguisher performance is a high-fit profile for deeper review. This sample does not assert that any specific contractor is eligible or intends to bid; eligibility and solicitation compliance must be confirmed by the contractor.

# BidWorth Research Sample — NOAA Ground Station as a Service

**Opportunity:** 1332KP26DGSAAS — Ground Station as a Service (GSaaS)
**Agency:** NOAA / NESDIS / OSPO
**Notice type:** Sources Sought / market research (not an RFP or RFQ)
**Latest observed notice:** Aug. 27, 2026
**Latest observed response deadline:** Sept. 11, 2026, 5:00 PM Eastern
**NAICS:** 517810 — All Other Telecommunications
**Latest observed set-aside:** None (superseding Aug. 26 version that showed Total Small Business)

> Research sample only. Before any paid advice or submission, re-open the latest official SAM.gov notice and reconcile all amendments/current attachments. Older versions conflict on deadline, page limit, and set-aside.

## Executive read

NOAA is using this notice to identify capable businesses for a prospective IDIQ supporting Ground Station as a Service. The contemplated work includes qualified ground-station sites, telemetry downlink, satellite commanding, scheduling, systems compatibility/proficiency support, security controls, and radio-frequency regulatory compliance.

This is **market research, not an award solicitation**. A contractor response should therefore prove capability against NOAA's stated criteria rather than present unsupported pricing or imply an award opportunity that does not yet exist.

## Critical version-control finding

The Aug. 26 and Aug. 27 notice versions materially differ. Publicly indexed records show the Aug. 26 version with a Sept. 9 deadline, Total Small Business set-aside, and a shorter capability-statement limit; the Aug. 27 version changes the deadline to Sept. 11 and removes the set-aside, while later reproductions show a 10-page capability-statement limit. **Use only the latest official SAM.gov record at response time.** Do not copy requirements from an older cached notice into a client deliverable without reconciliation.

## Capability gates

A prospective respondent should be treated as CONDITIONAL/NO-GO until evidence is supplied for:

1. **Ground sites:** sites meeting geographic, technical, availability, and satellite-communications requirements.
2. **Scheduling:** ability to accept and execute Government service requests under task-order scheduling interfaces.
3. **Performance:** evidence supporting the stated contact-success target, Eb/N0 margin, and rapid post-contact data delivery.
4. **Security:** artifacts showing applicable physical/cybersecurity controls and a framework acceptable to the Government.
5. **Regulatory:** ability to obtain, maintain, and comply with required RF licenses and local/international rules.
6. **Engineering support:** systems compatibility assessments and proficiency testing capability.
7. **Entity facts:** company identity, POC, UEI, active SAM registration, business size under NAICS 517810, and truthful socioeconomic certifications.
8. **OCI:** factual disclosure of known or potential organizational conflicts of interest.

## Client intake — evidence required

Do not draft capability claims from assumptions. Request: ground-site inventory and locations; antenna/RF bands and relevant technical specifications; historical contact-performance metrics; data-delivery architecture/latency evidence; security certifications or control artifacts actually held; RF licensing status; mission/satellite experience that can be substantiated; engineering-test examples; UEI/SAM status; business-size evidence; socioeconomic certifications actually held; and an OCI statement from the owner/legal authority.

## Response architecture

A concise capabilities statement can be organized as:

1. Company and SAM/UEI facts
2. GSaaS site footprint and technical capability
3. Scheduling and operational performance
4. Telemetry/downlink and commanding capability
5. Security and data handling
6. Regulatory/RF licensing
7. Engineering support and testing
8. Relevant substantiated examples
9. OCI disclosure
10. POC/contact details

Every factual capability assertion should map to client-supplied evidence. Unsupported past performance, certifications, licenses, site availability, security compliance, or mission history must not be invented.

## Go/no-go snapshot

**GO** only if the respondent can substantiate the core technical, site, security, licensing, SAM/UEI, and operational criteria.

**CONDITIONAL** if capability is plausible but evidence, licensing, site availability, security artifacts, or entity facts remain incomplete.

**NO-GO** if the response would require invented capabilities/certifications, cannot meet mandatory technical criteria, or the latest official notice is no longer active.

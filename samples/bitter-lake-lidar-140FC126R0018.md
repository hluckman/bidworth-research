**Purpose:** sanitized demonstration of BidWorth's solicitation triage, compliance extraction, and bid/no-bid research workflow. This is not a representation that BidWorth or any client is eligible to bid.

- Agency: U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, Construction A/E Team 1
- Notice: 140FC126R0018, NM Bitter Lake NWR Aerial Imaging
- Current response deadline: September 10, 2026, 5:00 PM Eastern
- Competition: Total Small Business set-aside
- NAICS: 541370 — Surveying and Mapping (except Geophysical) Services
- PSC: R404 — Support—Professional: Land Surveys—Cadastral (Non-Construction)
- Contract structure: Firm Fixed Price
- Place of performance: Bitter Lake National Wildlife Refuge, Roswell, New Mexico
- Response vehicle: SF330 qualifications submission; the notice expressly says this is not an RFP response.
- Current public records show Amendment 0001 and an added project-area boundary/map archive. The controlling official notice and every attachment/amendment must be rechecked immediately before any paid recommendation or submission.

### Scope extracted for qualification

The requirement covers topographic and bathymetric LiDAR and surveying work supporting Bitter Lake NWR. Public solicitation records describe approximately 40 square miles of LiDAR coverage, aerial imagery/orthophotography, detailed surveys of 20 water-control structures, processing/deliverables, and a seasonal LiDAR acquisition window between November 2026 and February 2027.

### Hard go/no-go gates

A prospective bidder should not advance until each item is evidenced from its own records:

1. Qualifies for the solicitation's Total Small Business set-aside and applicable NAICS size standard.
2. Has an active/award-eligible SAM registration as required by the solicitation.
3. Can truthfully document the professional qualifications, organization, key personnel, relevant projects and past performance requested through SF330.
4. Has the technical capability, equipment/subcontractor structure, licensed/professional resources where applicable, and capacity to perform the LiDAR, imagery and field-survey scope.
5. Can meet the required acquisition/performance schedule, including the seasonal flight window.
6. Has reviewed Amendment 0001, the boundary/project-area material, PWS, ranking criteria, wage determination and every other controlling attachment.
7. Has resolved any solicitation questions before the stated question cutoff shown in the controlling documents.

**BidWorth rule:** missing evidence is recorded as a gap, never filled with invented experience, certifications, staff, equipment, registrations or project history.

### SF330 response-work plan

**Step 1 — Amendment lock.** Download the latest official notice and attachments; build an amendment register and confirm the deadline/time zone.

**Step 2 — Compliance matrix.** Map each SF330/ranking requirement to the exact response section, owner, evidence source and status: ready / missing / clarification required.

**Step 3 — Evidence intake.** Collect only client-supplied or independently verifiable bidder facts: legal entity/SAM details, offices, disciplines, key personnel, licenses, equipment/resources, teaming/subconsultants, and relevant completed projects.

**Step 4 — Project selection.** Rank candidate past projects by similarity to bathymetric/topographic LiDAR, aerial imagery, survey complexity, refuge/environmental setting, acreage, schedule and federal performance. Do not imply relevance that source records do not support.

**Step 5 — Narrative drafting.** Draft concise factual narratives against the government's ranking criteria. Every claim must trace to intake evidence.

**Step 6 — Submission QA.** Check page/form constraints, signatures, required sections, amendment acknowledgement, filenames, email/submission instructions, deadline and attachment completeness.

### Fast client intake

To turn this brief into an executable compliance package, request: UEI/legal entity name; small-business status/NAICS eligibility; SF330 source material; key-personnel resumes; licenses; 5–10 potentially relevant projects with customer/scope/date/value and performance evidence; LiDAR/survey equipment and workflow; proposed subcontractors; quality-control approach; availability during the required acquisition window; and the bidder's downloaded official solicitation package.

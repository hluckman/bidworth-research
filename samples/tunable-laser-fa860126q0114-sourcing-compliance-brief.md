## Extracted technical sourcing screen

Current public notice summaries identify the requested system as a picosecond/tunable wavelength laser system and report characteristics including:

- broad tunable spectral coverage, publicly summarized as approximately 400–14,000 nm;
- tuning resolution/steps of no more than 1 nm over the stated 410–1150 nm range;
- linewidth no greater than 15 cm⁻¹;
- pulse repetition rate at least 50 Hz;
- pulse width below 1000 ps;
- pulse energy at least 1 µJ;
- pump laser(s), cables, power cords, controls and software included;
- packaging, shipping and FOB Destination delivery included;
- one-year manufacturer warranty; and
- on-site training by a factory-certified technician.

**Evidence rule:** each characteristic must be mapped to manufacturer literature or another authoritative technical source. A reseller statement alone should not be treated as proof where the solicitation calls for technical data.

## Pricing research checklist

A useful supplier quote should separate or expressly include:

1. base laser system;
2. required pump laser(s) and controls;
3. software/licensing;
4. accessories/cables/power components;
5. factory-certified on-site training and travel;
6. warranty;
7. packaging and freight to Wright-Patterson AFB;
8. any duties/tariffs or country-of-origin effects; and
9. lead time and quote-validity period.

Do not infer a government bid price from public list pricing. Final pricing and commercial commitments belong to the offeror.

Webscraped Apparel Data

RFQTotal Small BusinessNAICS 541910PSC R702

Live-status check

PostedSeptember 3–4, 2026 notice history.
Offer deadlineSeptember 11, 2026 at 5:00 PM ET (21:00 UTC).
QuestionsAmendment 0001 extends the question deadline to September 8, 2026 at 2:00 PM ET.
Contract structureFirm-fixed-price; one 12-month base period plus four 12-month option periods.

Requirement snapshot

BLS is seeking recurring structured web-scraped retail data covering apparel and related product categories from major U.S. retailers. Publicly indexed solicitation details identify Macy’s, Kohl’s, Nordstrom, Dillard’s and JCPenney as minimum retailer coverage and describe recurring collection approximately three times monthly. Expected fields include product identifiers/categories, offer and regular prices, seller information, shipping information where available, ranking indicators where available, and product descriptions/specifications. Delivery is expected in common structured electronic formats through secure transfer.

Submission signals

Indexed solicitation material describes separate technical-capability and price volumes. Technical material is expected to address approach, quality assurance, anticipated difficulties/mitigation, and a data dictionary. These are requirement signals only; an actual offeror should work from the current official solicitation and amendments before submission.

Bid/no-bid questions for a prospective contractor

  • Is the entity currently eligible for the stated small-business set-aside and registered as required?
  • Can it lawfully and reliably collect the required retailer data at the requested cadence for the full performance period?
  • Does its architecture handle retailer-site changes, anti-bot controls, data-quality checks, secure transfer, and repeatable schema management?
  • Can it support the minimum retailer coverage and required fields without relying on unverified assumptions?
  • Can it price a base year plus four option years while accounting for maintenance and retailer-site change risk?

Immediate next actions

  1. Open the current SAM.gov record and download the RFQ, Amendment 0001, and Q&A attachment.
  2. Confirm the exact evaluation basis, submission addresses, page/file constraints, representations, and amendment-acknowledgment requirements.
  3. Map every mandatory requirement to existing, truthful company evidence before drafting.
  4. Resolve questions before the September 8 question cutoff; do not rely on third-party summaries for submission.

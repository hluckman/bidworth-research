## 2. Immediate bid/no-bid gates

Before proposal drafting begins, collect and verify from the bidder:

- legal entity name and authorized signer;
- actual AI consulting/support capabilities relevant to the RFP;
- personnel/resume evidence required by the solicitation;
- truthful corporate experience and past-performance facts, if requested;
- ability to satisfy confidentiality/NDA requirements;
- ability to complete the official bid sheet accurately;
- any required insurance, security, data-handling, subcontracting, or conflict disclosures found in the controlling RFP;
- acceptance or escalation of contractual terms that require owner/legal review.

No missing bidder fact should be inferred or invented.

## 4. Compliance-matrix workflow

After receiving the controlling RFP, extract each requirement into a traceable matrix with these fields:

1. source section/page;
2. requirement verbatim or tightly paraphrased;
3. mandatory vs. evaluated vs. informational;
4. responsible proposal section;
5. bidder evidence needed;
6. owner/SME input needed;
7. status: confirmed / pending / gap / not applicable;
8. final QA evidence.

Special attention should be paid to submission method, deadline/time zone, proposal organization, page/file limits, pricing instructions, evaluation factors, minimum qualifications, representations, confidentiality requirements, and contractual exceptions.

## 5. Research package BidWorth can deliver

For a qualified bidder, the research-heavy front end can be performed remotely and asynchronously:

- official-document and amendment tracking;

Pricing, legal interpretations, contractual acceptance, certifications, representations, and bidder-specific claims remain owner-controlled inputs.

**Use:** sanitized procurement-research sample / contractor intake accelerator. This is not a representation that BidWorth or any client is eligible to bid.

- Official notice URL to control before advice/submission: https://sam.gov/opp/b278af2a9d604ffa83404c8951dc1287/view

**Control rule:** the current official SAM.gov notice, solicitation PDF/PWS, amendments, and incorporated clauses control. Public indexes are discovery/cross-check material only. Re-verify immediately before any paid advice, outreach, or submission support.

## Fast bid/no-bid gates

A contractor should not be moved to GO until BidWorth has evidence for each applicable gate:

1. Active SAM registration and small-business eligibility under NAICS 238290.
2. Demonstrable capability to dismantle industrial/rack-and-pinion elevator systems safely.
3. Hawaii/JBPHH access and mobilization feasibility for the required performance window.
4. Required licenses, insurance, safety program, qualified personnel, equipment and disposal pathway identified from the controlling documents.
5. Ability to satisfy any base-access, badging, security, environmental, hazardous-material, lockout/tagout, rigging, electrical-isolation, waste-handling and disposal requirements actually stated in the solicitation/PWS.
6. Price basis includes mobilization, labor, supervision, equipment, rigging, packaging, transport/disposal, insurance, taxes/fees if applicable, and contingency for access constraints.
7. No mandatory site-visit or question-deadline dependency remains unresolved. Public indexing reports a voluntary site visit and an August 27 question deadline; confirm against the current official record.

If any mandatory eligibility or access requirement cannot be evidenced, classify **NO-GO** rather than infer compliance.

## Document-control workflow

1. Open the official SAM notice and record the latest modification/amendment number and timestamp.
2. Download the solicitation, PWS and every amendment/attachment.
3. Build an amendment register: document, date, supersedes, bidder acknowledgement required, substantive change.
4. Extract all `shall`, `must`, `required`, `submit`, `include`, `provide`, deadline, page-limit and formatting requirements.
5. Separate bidder facts from government facts. BidWorth may summarize government requirements; the contractor must substantiate its own licenses, personnel, equipment, experience, registrations and certifications.
6. Reconcile quote instructions, evaluation basis, delivery/performance dates and points of contact against the latest amendment before final QA.

## Highest-value questions for contractor intake

- Are you currently active in SAM, and what size status do you represent under 238290?
- Have you dismantled elevators, rack-and-pinion lifts, industrial material-handling equipment, or comparable systems? Provide only verifiable project facts.
- Can your crew and equipment access JBPHH during the required window?
- What licenses, insurance, safety qualifications and manufacturer/elevator-specific competencies do you currently hold?
- What rigging/lifting equipment would you self-perform versus subcontract?
- What disposal/transport vendors or facilities would you use, if any, and are they already verified?
- What assumptions would materially change price?

## Final QA

Before a contractor relies on the brief: re-open the official notice; confirm the deadline and latest amendment; verify every required attachment; verify amendment acknowledgement; check quote destination and method; confirm all bidder-specific claims against supplied evidence; and mark unknowns as unknown rather than filling gaps by inference.

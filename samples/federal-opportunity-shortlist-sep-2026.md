# Client-facing prioritization method

BidWorth does **not** rank opportunities merely because keywords match. A paid shortlist is ranked on:

1. eligibility and set-aside fit;
2. capability/scope fit;
3. geography and delivery feasibility;
4. time remaining and amendment risk;
5. evidence burden (past performance, personnel, certifications, technical data);
6. pricing/mobilization complexity;
7. submission effort relative to realistic fit.

Each recommendation is delivered as **GO**, **CONDITIONAL**, or **NO-GO**, with unresolved bidder facts explicitly separated from verified solicitation facts.

## What a client receives

A typical bounded research engagement can produce a prioritized opportunity list with official links, deadlines, fit rationale, disqualifiers, missing bidder facts, amendment status, recommended next actions, and a lightweight tracker. BidWorth can then expand selected opportunities into a compliance matrix or deeper bid/no-bid brief without inventing credentials, certifications, past performance, pricing, or eligibility.

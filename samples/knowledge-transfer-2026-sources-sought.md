## Executive read

FTA is testing the market for vendors capable of Knowledge Transfer and Information Dissemination support. This notice does **not** itself result in an award. A useful response is a tailored technical capability statement, not a generic capability brochure. The current notice caps the package at 15 typed pages and asks respondents to substantiate three areas: recent relevant past performance, comparable corporate experience, and labor categories used on similar awarded work.

For a prospective contractor, the immediate go/no-go question is therefore evidentiary: can the firm truthfully document relevant work performed within the last three years and identify the requested contract details? If not, BidWorth would flag the opportunity rather than manufacture examples.

## Required response content

1. **Past performance within the last three years** — contract number, contract type, dollar value, point of contact, and concise description of work performed.
2. **Corporate experience** — evidence of work similar in nature, scope, complexity, and difficulty to the Statement of Objectives.
3. **Labor categories** — labor categories the firm actually used for this type of work under an awarded contract.
4. **Company identifiers** — company name, point of contact, address, phone number, CAGE code, and business size.
5. **Technical capability statement** — tailored to the SOO and kept within the 15-page maximum.

## Submission control

The current notice directs electronic submission to the named FTA contract specialist, with a copy to the secondary contracting contact, by **12:00 noon Eastern on Friday, September 11, 2026**. Contact information should be taken directly from the current official notice at submission time rather than from a stale bulk-data export.

## BidWorth go/no-go checklist

**GO only if the contractor can truthfully provide:**

- Relevant past performance from the last three years.
- The requested contract numbers, values/types and references/POCs.
- Comparable corporate experience tied to the SOO.
- Actual labor categories used on comparable awarded work.
- Current CAGE/business-size/company details.
- A substantive response tailored to the SOO rather than a stock brochure.

**HOLD / NO-GO if:**

- Required past-performance facts cannot be substantiated.
- The response would depend on invented contract history, personnel, credentials, or labor categories.
- The SOO cannot be mapped to demonstrated company capability.
- The notice has been amended, cancelled, superseded, or closed when rechecked immediately before delivery/submission.

## Suggested response architecture

1. Cover / company identification
2. Executive capability summary mapped to KTID objectives
3. SOO-to-capability crosswalk
4. Recent relevant past performance table
5. Comparable corporate experience
6. Labor-category mapping
7. Delivery / quality-control approach, using only client-supported facts
8. Closing and contracting contact information

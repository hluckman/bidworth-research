## Executive screen

AOC is researching the market for a future firm-fixed-price acquisition of Renewable Energy Credits (RECs). The contemplated requirement is 20,000 MWh annually for one base year plus four one-year options. This is a useful pursuit only for a company that can truthfully establish REC supply capability, Green-e qualification, required reporting/attestation controls, and relevant past performance. The present notice is an RFI; it does not request a binding price quotation.

## Scope extracted from the current notice

- Supply 20,000 MWh of qualifying RECs per year.
- Base period stated as September 28, 2026–September 27, 2027, followed by four option years.
- RECs must meet the Center for Resource Solutions Green-e Energy product definition and be Green-e certified.
- Renewable resources must satisfy the stated vintage/service-date rules.
- Contractor must support an annual renewable report verified/certified by an independent third-party auditor.
- Source-level evidence includes facility location, EPA eGRID subregion, REC type, available quantity, facility capacity, and production start date.
- Transaction controls include anti-double-sale attestations and conveyance of applicable environmental attributes.

## Immediate capability gates

**GO only if the prospective respondent can substantiate:**

1. Ability to source the required REC volume on the contemplated schedule.
2. Green-e-compliant/certified REC supply meeting the notice's resource and vintage requirements.
3. A credible independent third-party audit/reporting path.
4. Controls against double sale/double counting and ability to transfer required environmental attributes.
5. Source-facility data required by the notice.
6. Truthful answers regarding GSA Schedule status.
7. Verifiable similar-contract history if answering the Government's past-performance question.

**NO-GO / HOLD:** inability to substantiate any mandatory product qualification; uncertainty about REC ownership/retirement; inability to provide required attestations; or pressure to invent GSA/past-performance information.

## High-value clarification questions to evaluate

These are research prompts, not statements that should automatically be sent to the Government:

- Does AOC expect all 20,000 MWh for a contract year to be transferred in one transaction or may delivery be staged?
- What registry/retirement evidence will AOC accept in addition to the stated certificate notification and attestations?
- How will AOC evaluate a respondent without a GSA Schedule if it otherwise demonstrates full technical capability?
- Is the independent third-party audit expected to follow a specified standard or prescribed report format?
- How will the stated fiscal-year generation window interact with the September 28 contract start date for the base year?

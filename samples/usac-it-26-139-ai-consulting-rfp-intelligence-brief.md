## BidWorth deliverable for a prospective offeror

### 1. Solicitation control sheet
- controlling file name/version/date
- posting and closing dates
- question deadline and submission deadline, if distinct
- required submission channel
- amendment/Q&A log
- required acknowledgments and signatures

### 2. Compliance matrix
For every mandatory instruction or evaluated requirement:

| ID | Requirement | Source | Mandatory/Evaluated | Owner input needed | Draft evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | Offeror eligibility/qualification requirement | Current RFP | TBD after document review | Legal entity facts | Offeror-provided evidence only | OPEN |
| C-02 | Technical response requirement | Current RFP | TBD | Technical capability facts | Map factual evidence to requirement | OPEN |
| C-03 | Pricing/bid-sheet requirement | Attachment 1 | Mandatory if required by RFP | Owner-approved pricing | Populate only approved figures | OPEN |
| C-04 | Confidentiality requirement | Attachment 2 | Mandatory if required by RFP | Authorized signer decision | No signature by BidWorth | OPEN |
| C-05 | Submission format/deadline | Current RFP | Mandatory | None unless ambiguity exists | Final QA against controlling version | OPEN |

### 3. Evidence-gap register
Flag any requested claim that lacks client evidence, including:
- prior AI consulting engagements
- customer references or past performance
- personnel credentials
- security/privacy certifications
- technical platform partnerships
- staffing availability
- insurance or corporate representations

No gap is filled by inference. Missing evidence is marked **CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED**.

### 4. Technical response architecture
After the controlling RFP is reviewed, convert its evaluation structure into a response outline. Each section should contain only:
- the requirement being answered;
- the offeror's factual approach;
- evidence supplied by the offeror;
- measurable deliverables/milestones supported by the proposed approach;
- dependencies, assumptions, and exceptions requiring owner approval.

### 5. Price-package QA
BidWorth can check the bid sheet for completeness and consistency against owner-approved numbers, but does not choose pricing, margins, contractual assumptions, or negotiation positions. Those are owner decisions.

### 6. Final submission QA
Immediately before submission:
- re-check the official USAC page for changes;
- reconcile every amendment/Q&A against the matrix;
- verify required attachments are present;
- verify file names/formats/page limits;
- verify every mandatory field/signature;
- cross-check price figures across documents;
- confirm no unsupported qualification or past-performance claim entered the package;
- confirm the controlling deadline and submission route.

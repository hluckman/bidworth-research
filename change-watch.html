Know when the opportunity changes.

A federal solicitation can move after you first review it. BidWorth Change Watch tracks a defined opportunity for material public-record changes and turns amendments into a concise research update.

Request a Change Watch

What gets checked

Deadline changes

Updated response dates, question deadlines, site-visit dates, and other time-sensitive milestones found in current public records.

Amendments

New amendment notices and a plain-language change log of material updates visible in the solicitation record.

Evaluation & requirements

Changes to evaluation method, submission instructions, set-aside information, or material requirements when reflected in public documents.

Decision-ready update

A source-linked summary focused on what changed and what deserves another look—not a dump of raw notices.

Bounded research service

Useful after the first opportunity brief.

A Change Watch can be scoped to a specific solicitation and monitoring period. Deliverables, monitoring cadence, and pricing are confirmed before paid work begins. BidWorth does not submit offers, acknowledge amendments on a contractor's behalf, set bid pricing, make eligibility determinations, or represent a contractor to the Government.

BidWorth Research is an independent research service and is not a government agency. Public-record monitoring reduces manual checking but cannot guarantee that every agency update will be captured instantly. Contractors remain responsible for reviewing the official solicitation and meeting all submission requirements.

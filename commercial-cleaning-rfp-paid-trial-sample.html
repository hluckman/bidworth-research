Commercial Cleaning RFP Research — Paid Trial Sample

Sanitized capability sample. This is a demonstration of BidWorth Research's research format, not a claim of a prior client engagement, contract award, or completed government bid.

What a paid trial delivers

Open-opportunity verification
Confirm the solicitation is still accepting responses using the issuing agency's official source, not an aggregator alone.
Remote-execution screen
Flag mandatory walkthroughs, site visits, pre-bid meetings, local licensing, bonding, insurance, staffing, and submission-method constraints.
Fit summary
Extract scope, facility/location, contract term, set-aside/eligibility, deadline, submission method, required forms, and evaluation factors.
Bid/no-bid recommendation
Classify each opportunity as pursue, conditional, or no-go with a concise reason and next action.

Example opportunity record

Mandatory attendanceChecked explicitly. Mandatory in-person walkthrough or pre-bid meeting triggers a no-go unless the client confirms local coverage.

DeadlineDate, local time, time zone and amendment status recorded from the official notice.
RecommendationPURSUE / CONDITIONAL / NO-GO, with the decisive constraint stated in one sentence.

Research workflow

1. Search SAM.gov plus relevant state, county, municipal, school, housing-authority, transit, utility and other official procurement sources. 2. Open the issuing agency's live notice and solicitation documents. 3. Verify response status, amendments and deadline. 4. Screen for mandatory physical attendance and other disqualifiers. 5. Extract requirements into a compact opportunity brief. 6. Rank only realistic opportunities instead of forwarding an unfiltered RFP dump.

Trial handoff

A small paid trial can be delivered as a spreadsheet-style shortlist plus concise briefs. Each recommended opportunity includes its official source, verification date, deadline, fit rationale, major compliance risks and next action. No fabricated credentials, award history, certifications or client claims are used.

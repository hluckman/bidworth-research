Built for the research-heavy front end.

BidWorth starts with the controlling solicitation and current amendments, then organizes the public evidence that supports a proposal decision and drafting process. The result is designed to be handed directly to the person responsible for the final response.

Compliance map

Requirement-by-requirement mapping of instructions, evaluation factors, deliverables, deadlines, and submission constraints.

Risk register

Plain-language flags for solicitation clauses, missing evidence, unresolved bidder inputs, and potential compliance traps.

Market intelligence

Public-source research on relevant past awards, incumbents, competitors, agency buying patterns, and pricing evidence where available.

Proposal handoff

Concise differentiator/evidence notes, open questions, source links, and a prioritized list of issues to resolve before final drafting.

Evidence controls

No invented past performance. BidWorth does not fabricate contract wins, client experience, certifications, clearances, personnel qualifications, pricing inputs, or bidder representations. Client-specific facts remain clearly marked until supplied or approved by the client.

Bulk opportunity data may be used for discovery, but current solicitation facts are checked against current official or controlling sources before they are presented as verified. Where public evidence is incomplete, the deliverable says so rather than filling the gap with an assumption.

Typical delivery structure

1. Controlling-document and amendment check → 2. compliance matrix → 3. clause/risk review → 4. award/incumbent/competitor research → 5. agency and pricing intelligence → 6. evidence/differentiator notes → 7. source-linked handoff.

Scope depends on solicitation size and the public information available. BidWorth provides independent research and drafting support, not legal advice, government representation, or an award guarantee.

BidWorth Research

RFP intelligence without the noisy lead dump.

A bounded research pilot for firms that need active RFP/RFQ opportunities found, verified, qualified, summarized, and organized before senior staff invest pursuit time.

What the pilot produces
Verified opportunitiesCurrent, legitimate, still-open opportunities checked against the issuing source before they enter the shortlist.
Fit screeningScope, geography, eligibility, deadlines, mandatory requirements, and obvious disqualifiers mapped to your stated capabilities.
Decision briefsConcise pursuit notes explaining why an opportunity appears worth review—or why it should be passed.
Usable trackingA structured pipeline with source, discovery date, deadline, status, qualification notes, and next action.

Designed for a paid pilot, not an open-ended commitment

The engagement can begin with a fixed research window and an agreed target profile. The output is a review-ready batch of qualified opportunities plus the evidence used to verify them. Continuation is an owner decision after the pilot; there is no requirement to commit to ongoing staffing.

Research can include

  • Federal, state, local, corporate, nonprofit, association, foundation, education, and healthcare RFP/RFQ sources when relevant to the client target profile.
  • Solicitation and amendment review, deadline validation, qualification notes, and compliance-oriented requirement extraction.
  • Organization and award-context research where reliable public evidence is available.
  • Proposal-support preparation such as compliance matrices, checklists, structured outlines, and document inventories using client-approved facts and materials.

What BidWorth does not fabricate

No invented contract wins, client history, certifications, clearances, past performance, incumbent knowledge, or unverifiable contacts. Bulk solicitation data is treated as discovery material; current official records control before an opportunity is represented as live.

Start with a target profile

Provide the services you sell, target geography, deal-size preferences, exclusions, certifications or eligibility that are actually held, and any preferred opportunity sources. BidWorth can then structure the research around those constraints.

BidWorth Research

Security Procurement Qualification Sprint

A bounded research deliverable for security companies that need public-sector opportunities screened before spending bid time.

What you receive

  • Official-source verification of each RFP, RFQ, IFB, Sources Sought, or solicitation.
  • Deadline, place of performance, set-aside, NAICS, mandatory meeting, and submission-route checks.
  • Requirement screening for licensing, insurance, bonding, staffing, experience, and other visible gates.
  • A decision label for each opportunity: Prime, Subcontractor, Teaming, or No-Go.
  • A concise next-action note and source links so your team can validate the decision.

Best fit

Guard services, patrol, event security, access control, security staffing, and adjacent contractors that want a smaller decision-ready shortlist rather than an unfiltered opportunity dump.

Research standard

Bulk feeds and aggregators may be used for discovery, but a solicitation is not presented as current until its controlling official record is checked. BidWorth does not claim client wins, certifications, licenses, clearances, or guaranteed awards.

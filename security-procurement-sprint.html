Security Procurement Qualification Sprint

A bounded research deliverable for security companies that need current public-sector opportunities screened for actual bid fit—not a raw portal dump.

What you receive

  • A prioritized shortlist of current federal, state, county, city, transit, airport, utility, housing-authority, school-district, and other relevant public-sector opportunities.
  • Official-source verification of each included opportunity at research time.
  • Deadline, place of performance, solicitation type, set-aside, licensing, insurance, bonding, staffing, mandatory-meeting, and other material requirement screening when disclosed.
  • A clear Prime / Subcontractor / Teaming / No-Go classification with the reason and next action.
  • Source links and concise research notes suitable for an owner or capture lead to review quickly.

Designed to prevent wasted bid effort

The sprint emphasizes qualification over volume. Bulk procurement data may be used for discovery, but a record is not presented as current merely because it appears in a bulk feed. Included opportunities are checked against the controlling public source before delivery.

What BidWorth does not claim

BidWorth does not represent itself as your attorney, contracting officer, licensed security consultant, or guarantor of award. We do not invent past performance, certifications, licenses, clearances, contract wins, or eligibility. Final bid/no-bid and contractual decisions remain with the client.

Useful intake information

Service geography; guard types/services offered; licenses held; insurance/bonding limits; staffing capacity; target contract size; socioeconomic certifications (if any); and any agencies or opportunity types you want included or excluded.

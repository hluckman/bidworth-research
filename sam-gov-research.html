What the research can include

Opportunity sourcing

A screened shortlist based on capability fit, NAICS, geography, set-aside constraints, response dates, and other stated requirements.

Live notice verification

Current solicitation status, response deadline, amendments, and controlling public record are checked before a deliverable is treated as current.

Solicitation analysis

Scope, submission requirements, evaluation factors, attachments, questions deadlines, and practical disqualifiers organized into a usable brief.

Compliance mapping

Requirements are mapped into a structured checklist so missing bidder facts, documents, certifications, or owner decisions are visible rather than guessed.

Two ways to start

Already have a solicitation? Send the official notice or solicitation identifier plus the decision you need to make. Research can focus on bid/no-bid, compliance extraction, amendment review, supplier research, or a writer-ready evidence package.

Need opportunities found? Send what your business can actually provide, where it can perform, relevant NAICS or product/service categories if known, and any eligibility constraints. The output can be a ranked, evidence-backed opportunity shortlist rather than a raw notice dump.

Verification comes before recommendation.

Bulk SAM.gov data, search indexes, aggregators, and cached pages are useful for discovery, but they are not treated as the controlling solicitation record. Before paid advice or a current-status conclusion, BidWorth re-checks the official/current notice and available amendments. Bidder-specific qualifications, certifications, past performance, clearance, registrations, and capabilities are never invented.

Designed for bounded project work.

BidWorth provides independent procurement research rather than salaried staffing or an outsourced government representative. Scope and pricing are confirmed before paid work begins. The contractor remains responsible for bidder representations, legal and compliance judgments, pricing authority, certifications, and final submission decisions.

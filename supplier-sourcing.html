What you receive

A source-backed supplier comparison built around your actual requirements.

  • Supplier identity and capability evidence
  • Manufacturer / distributor / reseller status where verifiable
  • Product or service fit against required specifications
  • Location and service-area checks
  • MOQ, lead time, certifications and pricing evidence when publicly available
  • Contact route verification
  • Risk flags and unresolved-data fields
  • Ranked shortlist with research notes

Research controls

Unknown information stays unknown until it can be verified.

  • No invented prices, lead times or certifications
  • No guessed email addresses
  • No claim that a supplier is authorized without evidence
  • Source URLs retained for material findings
  • Conflicting information is flagged, not silently resolved
  • Client retains negotiation, purchasing and contractual authority

Good fit for

One-time supplier searches, RFQ preparation, alternate-source research, distributor/manufacturer identification, government-procurement supply chains, and vendor comparison projects that can be completed from public or client-provided information.

Not included: placing orders, signing supplier agreements, making legal/compliance representations, or claiming client qualifications. BidWorth provides research and decision support.

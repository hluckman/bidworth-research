Built for procurement research projects

Typical assignments begin with a live RFP, RFQ, IFB, Sources Sought notice, or DIBBS/SAM.gov requirement. The work stays bounded to research and decision support so the client retains pricing, negotiation, representations, and submission authority.

  • Review the current solicitation and amendments supplied by the client or available from an official source
  • Extract products/services, quantities, specifications, delivery constraints, certifications, and mandatory supplier conditions
  • Identify manufacturers, distributors, vendors, or subcontractors that appear capable of meeting the requirement
  • Compare public pricing, catalog, lead-time, authorization, service-area, and specification evidence where available
  • Separate verified facts from items that require supplier confirmation
  • Prepare a quotation-ready contact list using only verified public/company-controlled contact routes
  • Return a ranked supplier comparison with risks, missing facts, and recommended next checks

Deliverable

  • Solicitation requirement summary
  • Supplier shortlist and source links
  • Requirement-by-requirement comparison
  • Pricing/lead-time evidence when public
  • Contact-route verification
  • Open questions for quotation outreach
  • Compliance and disqualifier flags
  • Approve / reject / needs-more-research notes

Research controls

  • Official/current solicitation records are verified before requirement-level advice
  • Bulk SAM data is treated as discovery-only
  • No invented supplier quotes, certifications, authorization, lead times, or past performance
  • No guessed email addresses
  • No representation that a vendor has agreed to price or perform unless documented
  • Conflicts and unknowns remain explicitly flagged

Useful when you already know the opportunity but not the supply path

This service is especially suited to government supply bids, facilities/service procurements, alternate-source searches, quotation research, subcontractor identification, and fast bid/no-bid diligence. It can also support a paid freelance test built around one real solicitation without requiring access to the client's purchasing accounts.

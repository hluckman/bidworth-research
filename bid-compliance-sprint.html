BidWorth Research

Bid Compliance Sprint

A bounded research deliverable for contractors who already have a government RFQ/RFP and need the solicitation turned into an actionable submission plan.

Send one live solicitation. Receive a structured compliance package built from the current official notice and controlling documents.

What the sprint covers

  • Current notice, amendment, deadline, NAICS and set-aside verification
  • Submission instructions and required-document extraction
  • Compliance matrix mapping requirements to response items
  • Evaluation-factor and bid/no-bid risk summary
  • Questions, site-visit, registration and acknowledgement gates
  • Missing-input checklist for items only the contractor can supply
  • Organized source links and amendment/change notes

What it does not claim

BidWorth does not invent past performance, certifications, registrations, personnel credentials, pricing, contract wins, or other contractor facts. Contractor-supplied claims remain contractor-controlled. Final legal, pricing, certification and submission decisions stay with the contractor.

Best fit

Federal RFQs/RFPs and other public-sector solicitations where the buyer documents are available online and the immediate need is research, requirement extraction, compliance organization, or proposal preparation support.

FEDERAL PROCUREMENT RESEARCH

BidWorth Research

BidWorth Research provides independent research support for businesses pursuing U.S. government contracting opportunities. We help turn public procurement information into concise, decision-ready research.

Services

  • SAM.gov opportunity monitoring and research
  • Solicitation review and bid/no-bid research briefs
  • Federal contractor and market research
  • Supplier and vendor sourcing research
  • Opportunity qualification and deadline verification

How engagements work

Research is provided on a project or agreed-scope basis. Deliverables and pricing are confirmed before paid work begins. BidWorth Research does not guarantee contract awards and does not represent itself as a government agency or contracting authority.

Customer support

Questions about an engagement, deliverable, payment, cancellation, or refund can be sent to luckmanhayden@gmail.com.

Cancellation & refunds

Work may be cancelled before research begins for a full refund of any amount paid for that work. Once custom research has begun, refund requests are evaluated based on work already completed. If BidWorth Research cannot provide an agreed deliverable, amounts paid for the undelivered portion will be refunded. Any billing dispute should be raised promptly using the customer-support email above.

Privacy

Information supplied for a research engagement is used to perform and communicate about that engagement. Sensitive payment-card information is handled by the applicable payment processor and is not requested by BidWorth Research through this website.

What we do

Research built around the bid decision.

Instead of forwarding a feed of procurement notices, BidWorth narrows public information into research a contractor can use to decide where to spend its time.

01 / OPPORTUNITY

Opportunity Intelligence

Monitoring and qualification based on capability fit, scope, location, response dates, set-asides, and practical bid factors. See SAM.gov research.

02 / SOLICITATION

Solicitation Analysis

Structured review of notices and solicitation materials, including requirements, deadlines, constraints, and bid/no-bid research notes. See the fixed-scope compliance matrix service.

03 / MARKET

Supplier & Market Research

Vendor, supplier, contractor, and market research to support sourcing decisions and improve understanding of an opportunity.

Project-based engagements

Research is provided on an agreed-scope basis. Deliverables and pricing are confirmed before paid work begins. Work cancelled before research begins is eligible for a full refund. Once custom research has begun, refund requests are evaluated based on work already completed. If an agreed deliverable cannot be provided, amounts paid for the undelivered portion will be refunded.

Independent research

BidWorth Research is an independent research service and is not a government agency or contracting authority. We do not guarantee contract awards. Information supplied for an engagement is used to perform and communicate about that engagement. Payment-card information is handled by the applicable payment processor and is not requested through this website.

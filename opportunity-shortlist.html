A bounded research project for businesses that need relevant government opportunities identified, screened, and prioritized without paying for a long-term monitoring engagement.

Request a Shortlist

What you receive

  • Relevant federal, state, or local RFP/RFQ opportunities based on the capabilities you provide
  • Current official or agency-controlled source links where available
  • Response deadlines and key submission timing
  • Eligibility, set-aside, location, and major requirement flags
  • Concise scope and fit notes
  • A prioritized list with pursue / review / pass recommendations

How we screen

Search results and bulk procurement data are treated as discovery inputs, not final truth. Candidate opportunities are checked against current official records before they are presented as active. Conflicts, missing attachments, or unresolved amendment questions are flagged instead of guessed.

Designed for a decision, not a link dump

The deliverable is organized so you can quickly see why an opportunity may or may not fit. Research can incorporate the capabilities, geography, NAICS/PSC preferences, certifications, set-aside eligibility, contract size, and exclusions you provide. BidWorth does not invent qualifications, registrations, certifications, past performance, or eligibility.

Bounded project scope

This service is intended for a one-time research request. Scope and pricing are confirmed before paid work begins. BidWorth provides independent procurement research and decision support; it does not guarantee awards, provide legal advice, or represent a client before a government agency.

Bid/no-bid checks

  • Confirm current SAM.gov registration and small-business eligibility under NAICS 333111 before relying on the set-aside.
  • Map the offered mower models line-by-line against the solicitation's salient specifications, especially tractor compatibility, dimensions, hydraulics/PTO, controls, and required delivery timing.
  • Re-check the official notice immediately before submission for amendments or changed deadlines.
  • Resolve any specification ambiguity before the questions cutoff; do not assume a commercial model is technically acceptable merely because it is marketed as a flail mower.
  • Confirm freight, FOB/delivery obligations, lead time, warranty documentation, and all representations/certifications required by the RFQ.

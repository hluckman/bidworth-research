What the research can include

Opportunity screening

Research open opportunities against product, NSN, FSC/PSC, quantity, due date, set-aside, delivery, and other stated filters supplied by the client.

Solicitation review

Organize publicly available solicitation facts, amendments, technical-document references, stated certifications, packaging or delivery requirements, and deadline information.

Supplier sourcing

Research manufacturers, distributors, approved-source evidence, CAGE information, and publicly verifiable supplier contact channels where available.

Public award research

Research relevant public award history, prior suppliers, pricing signals when publicly available, and other context useful to a contractor's own quote decision.

Scope boundary: BidWorth provides independent research. We do not claim DIBBS contract wins, do not submit quotes as the contractor, do not certify eligibility, and do not guarantee awards. Current solicitation records should be verified before a client relies on them for a bid decision.

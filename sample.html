Concise summary of the work requested, major performance requirements, location constraints, and other facts that determine whether the opportunity deserves deeper review.

Qualification factors

Capability fit, likely NAICS alignment, set-aside eligibility, geographic fit, timing, mandatory registrations, and material solicitation requirements are surfaced rather than buried in attachments.

Deadline & document check

Current status and response dates are checked against the official live record before being presented as actionable. Bulk procurement data is treated as discovery material, not proof that an opportunity remains open.

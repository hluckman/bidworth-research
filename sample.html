Live Federal Opportunity Research Sample

A compact example of how BidWorth turns a live federal procurement into decision-ready research. Official agency material is treated as controlling; third-party intelligence is labeled separately and never substituted for the solicitation.

Competition
Unrestricted; award limited to U.S. firms or U.S. individuals

What the official source establishes

Verified USTDA currently lists the procurement as open through September 8. The agency describes the work as project-scoping support for decision-making on advanced exploration, extraction, processing, associated infrastructure and related mining-sector projects across multiple world regions.

Amendment control

Corroborated Current procurement intelligence reports Amendment 00002 posted August 25 with Government answers to bidder questions and no change to the September 8 deadline. Open item Individual Q&A answers should not be converted into compliance requirements until the official amendment attachment itself is inspected.

Immediate eligibility / compliance gates

  • Confirm the offeror satisfies the U.S.-firm/U.S.-individual award limitation.
  • Confirm active SAM registration through submission, award and performance as required by the solicitation.
  • Control subcontracting: reported solicitation terms limit subcontractor use to less than 50% of proposed price.
  • Acknowledge applicable amendments and reconcile the base solicitation, A00001 and A00002 before final compliance mapping.

Research judgment

Analytical The useful deliverable is not a generic summary. It is a controlled evidence trail: requirement → source → status → owner/action. Any incumbent, competitor or historical-price lead remains separately labeled until verified against an authoritative award source.

Bid/no-bid research note

Proceed to deeper review only if the eligibility gates clear. Next research should inspect the controlling amendment/Q&A attachment, build a requirement-level compliance matrix, verify analogous awards and normalize historical pricing by scope before using it as a benchmark.

Source basis: USTDA's current Project Scoping Opportunities and opportunity page, cross-checked August 26, 2026. Third-party amendment intelligence is explicitly identified above. This sample is research demonstration only—not legal advice, a compliance certification, or a guarantee of award.
